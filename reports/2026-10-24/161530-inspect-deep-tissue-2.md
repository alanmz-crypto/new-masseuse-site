<div class="wp-block-uagb-advanced-heading uagb-block-d1bf4422"><h2 class="uagb-heading-text">What Is Deep Tissue Massage?</h2><p class="uagb-desc-text">Deep tissue massage uses slow, deliberate pressure on muscles that stay tight. I begin by asking about your goals and how much pressure you like, then check in as I work. Deep pressure is never required: you can ask me to lighten up at any time, and I will.<br><br>Research suggests deep tissue massage added to conventional rehabilitation may reduce pain intensity and improve some neck-movement and quality-of-life measures in people with neck pain linked to tender spots in the muscles.<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36645811/">[1]</a> A randomized trial in athletes linked repeated deep tissue massage with improvements in flexibility and recovery-related measures, though the results may not apply to every client.<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40510040/">[2]</a> Trials in specific musculoskeletal conditions suggest deep tissue massage may improve pain or movement-related outcomes for some people, but results vary by condition and outcome, and long-term pain benefits remain uncertain.<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36645811/">[1]</a><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31655314/">[3]</a><a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29861215/">[4]</a></p></div>
