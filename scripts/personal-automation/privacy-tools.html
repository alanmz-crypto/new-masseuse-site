Last updated: October 20, 2026. This policy covers Ryan Personal Automation, operated by Ryan Steitz, and its related Google connections for personal tasks and authorized business administration, including Thai Solitude. Each account holder chooses which services to connect and which tasks to authorize.

Google data accessed and why

  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: file contents, names, folders, metadata, document text and spreadsheet values are used for backup, organization, drafting and record keeping. Depending on the granted permission, the tools can read, create, edit, download, move or delete files and manage sharing. Full Drive permission can reach existing files across the account; it is not restricted to a business folder. Access is used for the authorized task. A configured sync can delete destination files when their source files are deleted.
  • Gmail: message contents, sender and recipient details, subjects and labels are used to find correspondence, summarize it and prepare replies. Enabled tools can send messages, change labels or move messages to Trash when those actions are explicitly authorized. The current Gmail permission does not allow permanent deletion.
  • Calendar: calendar and event details, including titles, descriptions, times, locations and participants, are used to check availability and organize appointments. Creating, changing or deleting events and responding to invitations require an authorized task.
  • Contacts: contact details, such as names, email addresses and phone numbers, are used to find or maintain contact records. Any change requires an authorized task.
  • Search Console, when connected: accessible property information and Thai Solitude’s search performance, sitemap and URL indexing reports are used to understand how the site appears in Google Search. The connection requests read-only access. It does not submit sitemaps, change property settings or publish website changes.

Google account information may also be read to check that the intended account is connected. A Google permission can allow more operations than a particular tool exposes. Consent to a permission is separate from the instructions for an individual task.

Storage, sharing and AI-assisted tasks

Authorization tokens and tool configuration are stored in access-restricted files on Ryan’s computer. The tools communicate with Google to perform authorized tasks. Selected files, exports and working records may be stored locally or in the authorized Google account. Operational logs may contain file paths, names, transfer results and errors. People with access to a shared file, folder, calendar or message can see the information shared with them.

When an account holder authorizes an AI-assisted task, selected Google data returned by the connected tools may be included in the assistant’s conversation and sent to the AI service used for that task. This can include relevant message text, contact or event details, document content, spreadsheet values or website reports. The provider and its account settings govern processing and retention by that service. The account holder should agree to that destination before data is sent. Google authorization tokens are not included in those conversations.

Google data is used only for the authorized workflow. The operator does not sell it, use it for advertising or use it to train generalized AI models. AI-assisted use must be configured consistently with these limits and Google’s Limited Use requirements. An rclone backup alone does not send file contents to an AI service. Other people receive data only as part of an authorized action, such as sending a message, inviting an attendee or sharing a file.

Google data and access tokens are not stored on these public information pages. Visiting the pages is subject to the separate website privacy policy, including ordinary hosting logs. This app policy does not describe or replace Thai Solitude’s handling of information provided directly for a massage appointment.

Retention, removal and revoking access

Files and records remain in their configured locations until an authorized user or a configured task removes them. Local logs, working copies and backups have no fixed automatic deletion period. Google Trash, earlier backups and assistant conversation histories can retain copies after an item is removed elsewhere. Connected providers apply their own retention settings and policies.

The account holder can revoke future access through Google Account connections. Revocation does not delete copies already made or messages already sent. Contact Ryan to request removal of stored tokens, local logs or working copies, or help locating retained copies. Data held by Google or an AI provider may also need to be removed through that provider’s controls.

Changes and Google API data policy

A new service, purpose or sharing destination requires an updated disclosure and the account holder’s authorization before it is used. Ryan Personal Automation’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
