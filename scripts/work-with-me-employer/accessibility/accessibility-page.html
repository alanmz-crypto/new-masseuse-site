Thai Solitude aims to make this website accessible and usable for everyone. I’ve reviewed the site for accessibility and will continue to make improvements as needed.

What has been checked

In October 2026 the site was tested with an automated accessibility checker against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Levels A and AA. It was also checked for keyboard use, text contrast, and how the pages look on a phone and when zoomed in. This was not a full audit by an accessibility specialist, and automated testing cannot find every barrier, so some parts of the site may still be hard to use.

Every page has a “Skip to content” link, one main heading, and text descriptions for its images, and the pages can be zoomed in without side-scrolling.

A button in the header switches the site between light and dark colors. Your choice is saved in your browser, and the button can be used with a keyboard. Text contrast and keyboard focus were checked in both the light and the dark version.

The collapsible research summaries on the Thai Massage, Therapeutic Massage, Deep Tissue Massage and Chair Massage pages open and close with a mouse or with the Enter and Space keys, and their text contrast was checked in both the light and the dark version.

The Chair Massage research summaries and Sources panel were included in the mouse, Enter and Space keyboard, and light- and dark-version text-contrast checks.

Known limitations

The map on the Contact page is provided by Google, so how it works with assistive technology is up to Google, and it keeps its own light colors in dark mode. The email address and phone number on that page are also available as text.

On the For Practices page, two links inside the expandable sections may not be announced clearly by screen readers.

No screen reader was used in this testing. That includes the research summaries on the Thai Massage, Therapeutic Massage, Deep Tissue Massage and Chair Massage pages, which are standard collapsible sections built into the browser and were tested with a mouse and a keyboard.
