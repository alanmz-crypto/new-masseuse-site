What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts, no ads, and no tracking tools. It does not ask you for personal information, and it does not set cookies for visitors.

If you contact me

If you email or call me, I receive whatever you choose to share, such as your name, your email address or phone number, and your message. I use it only to reply and to arrange an appointment. I do not sell it or share it for marketing.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by SiteGround, which keeps routine server records, such as visitors' IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to display them.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, such as the Oregon license lookup and Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Questions

To ask what information I have about you, or to have it deleted, email butsabad@gmail.com.
