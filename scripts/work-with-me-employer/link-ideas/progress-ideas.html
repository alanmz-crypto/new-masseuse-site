Progress navigation ideas

Five ways to show how far along the page you are and what each section is, so you know where a tap will take you. Pick one, then scroll, tap or slide your thumb.

1. Growing segments

One bar per section, sized by how long the section is. The section you are in grows and shows its name; the others shrink to their number. On a tablet every name fits.

2. Bar that opens a list

A slim bar says where you are (“2 of 6 · Common reasons”). Tap it and a sheet slides up with every section: its name, a short description, its length and its own progress, with ticks for what you have read.

3. Now and Next

A ring shows progress through the section you are reading, and a button names the next one (“Next: Who can benefit”). Tap the name to open a timeline of all the steps.

4. Scrubber

Slide your thumb along the bar, like scrubbing a video. A bubble shows the section under your thumb and what it covers; let go to jump there. A single tap works too.

5. Edge rail

A thin rail on the right edge, one piece per section, filling as you read; the current section's name sits beside it. Touch and slide along the rail and every name fans out; let go to jump.
