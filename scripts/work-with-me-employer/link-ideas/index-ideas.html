Index icon ideas

A small index icon sits right above the side rail. Press and hold it: every section name slides out of the rail. Lift your finger: they slide back in and disappear. The page does not move. Pick a design, scroll a little, then press and hold the icon.

1. Fan

Icon: three short lines (a list). Each name slides sideways out of its own piece of the rail, one after another from top to bottom, so every name sits right next to the part of the rail it belongs to.

2. Unfold

Icon: three dots in a column. A dark list unfolds downward out of the icon, like a window blind, with every section in order and the one you are reading in green.

3. Drop

Icon: numbered squares. The names drop out of the icon and land beside their own piece of the rail with a small bounce; on release they fly back up into the icon.

4. Panel

Icon: a bookmark. The rail itself widens into a light panel from the edge of the screen and the names fade in on it, each beside its piece of the rail.

5. Arc

Icon: a small compass circle. The names swing out along a gentle curve that hugs the edge of the screen, the middle ones reaching furthest, like a fan of cards.
