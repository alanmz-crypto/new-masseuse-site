Side rail ideas

The strip at the top only shows where you are and the current topic. The rail on the right edge, where your thumb already is, does the choosing. Slide along it to browse the sections without losing your place, and jump only when you choose. After any jump, “Back to …” returns you to where you were reading.

1. Slide, lift, then choose

Slide up and down the rail: a card beside your thumb names each section and what it covers. Lift your thumb and the card stays, with “Go there” and “Stay here”. Nothing moves until you tap Go.

2. Slide, then pull in to go

Slide to browse. To jump, pull your thumb in toward the middle of the screen until the card turns green, then let go. Lift without pulling and you stay where you are.

3. Slide to peek at the page

Sliding shows a small preview of the section itself: its heading, its description and the start of its text. Lift and the preview stays; tap it to go there, or tap anywhere else to stay.

4. Slide, then hold to go

Slide to browse. Rest your thumb on a section and a ring fills; keep holding until it is full and the page goes there. Keep moving or lift early and nothing happens.

5. Pull out a drawer

A short tab on the right edge shows how far you have read. Tap it or swipe it left and a drawer slides in with every section, its description, length and progress. Scroll the drawer freely; the page only moves when you tap a section. Swipe the drawer right to close.
