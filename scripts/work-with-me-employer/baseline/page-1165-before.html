The person behind the work

From export sales to massage.

Before massage, I worked in export sales and customer service for Thai manufacturers. That work taught me to listen carefully, explain things plainly, and follow through on what I promise. I bring the same habits to my clients.

Since 2020, I have worked in a Thai bodywork salon, in a busy Chicago massage practice, and in a practice of my own. At Windy City Massage, my clients have included hotel guests and business travelers, people I might see only once. When a session may be the only one, the first conversation matters.

My approach

Listen. Assess. Adapt.

I begin with what brought someone in, then let their comfort and feedback guide the work.

01

Listen first

A conversation about goals, preferences, relevant history, and what would make the session useful.

02

Notice and respond

Observe comfort and areas of tension within massage scope, then invite feedback during the work.

03

Make it individual

Adjust pressure, pacing, focus, and movement instead of following the same routine for everyone.
