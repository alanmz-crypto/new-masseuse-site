The person behind the work

A career shaped by people and practice.

Before massage, Butsaba worked in export sales and customer service for Thai manufacturers. That experience informs the way she listens, communicates clearly, and follows through with clients.

Since 2020, she has worked in a Thai bodywork studio, a busy Chicago massage setting, and a practice of her own. At Windy City Massage, her clients included hotel guests and business travelers. When the time together may be a single visit, the opening conversation matters.

Her approach

Listen. Assess. Adapt.

Butsaba begins with what brought someone in, then lets their comfort and feedback guide the work.

01

Listen first

A conversation about goals, preferences, relevant history, and what would make the session useful.

02

Notice and respond

Observe comfort and areas of tension within massage scope, then invite feedback during the work.

03

Make it individual

Adjust pressure, pacing, focus, and movement instead of following the same routine for everyone.
