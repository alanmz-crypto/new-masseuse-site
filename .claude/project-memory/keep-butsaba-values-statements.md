On 2026-10-13 a first-person prose pass rewrote the About page's "Learn About Thai Solitude" paragraph and its three accordion items ("My Commitment to Your Wellness", "Massage as Part of Everyday Care", "Values That Guide Every Session") into tips for the reader ("Please Speak Up", "No Reason Needed", "How I Check In"). **Why:** On the About page, those passages are Butsaba describing herself. They were already first person, so changing them was not a voice fix. It changed what they say.

**How to apply:** In copy passes on this site, keep any passage that is already first person and states her aims or values. Only touch it for a specific defect, such as a banned term or a fact error, and show Ryan the proposal first. Rewrite freely only where the copy is third person or boilerplate.
