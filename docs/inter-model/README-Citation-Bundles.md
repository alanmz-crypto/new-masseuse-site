## How to use this chain

1. **Start at the register**, not at a bundle. The registers are short and current.
2. **Open a bundle only for the source text**, using the table above to pick the right one.
3. **If bundle 1 or 2 contradicts bundle 4 or 5, the later bundle wins.**
4. **Do not restate a bundle's evidence into a new file.** That creates a sixth copy that
   can drift, which is what this chain already suffers from.

## One caveat on the bundles themselves

Every source in them was re-verified against its abstract or publisher record during this
work. Where a bundle's description of a study differed from the study's own abstract, the
abstract governs. Those differences are recorded in `Thai Solitude - Citation Worklist.md`
section 5.

**S18 (Cook 2015)** is cited by bundle 5 and could **not** be located; treat it as
unverified until found.
