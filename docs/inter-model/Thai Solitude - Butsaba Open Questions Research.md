## 3. Pricing (item 1.4)

**Outcome: Partly answerable. A defensible range only.**

Portland market context gathered in this session:

| Source | Indication |
|--------|-----------|
| Portland City Massage (local rate page) | **$130 for 60 minutes**, rising to $140 from Nov 1 |
| National and local aggregators | $60 to $150 for 60 min; $90 to $200 for 90 min |
| Portland LMT hourly **salary** | ~$36.55/hour employed |

**The important distinction:** an employed LMT earns ~$36/hour; an **independent
practitioner sets a session rate** that has to cover room rent, insurance, booking and
non-contact time. A solo practitioner in a rented room is typically in the
**$90-$140 for 60 minutes** band in Portland.

⚠️ **Do not publish a price from this.** It is a market range, not her decision, and a
published price is a commitment. But it is useful for two things:

1. It tells you the site does not need pricing at launch to look credible.
2. It gives Butsaba a benchmark if she wants one.

**Still `[ASK]`.** Pricing is a business decision, not an industry-standard fact.

## 6. Facility permit for a rented room (item 1.12)

**Outcome: Answerable - already answered. See the Compliance Register.**

This is not really an industry-standard question; it is a legal one, and the answer is
already recorded:

- A facility owned or operated by a person licensed by a health professional regulatory
  board is **exempt** - and **ORS 676.160(6)** lists the State Board of Massage
  Therapists as such a board.
- So a sole LMT's own room is **likely exempt**, and the live question is **who owns or
  operates the rented room**.
- Draft letter to the Board is in the Compliance Register.

**Nothing here depends on Butsaba.** It depends on the Board.
