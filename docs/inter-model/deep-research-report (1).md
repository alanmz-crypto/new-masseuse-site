## What Is Therapeutic Massage?  

Therapeutic massage is not just a generic rubdown – it’s **focused, goal-oriented bodywork**. Our approach combines relaxation with hands-on techniques to address your concerns. For example, if you’re experiencing muscle pain or postural tension, we may incorporate gentle stretching and trigger-point release.  The Cleveland Clinic explains that “therapeutic massage involves focused work on your body’s soft tissue” by trained therapists, often going deeper than spa massage to resolve issues.  

## Common Reasons Clients Choose Therapeutic Massage  

People often book therapeutic massage for **everyday stiffness and stress relief**. It’s also popular for:  
- **Neck & shoulder tension:** Desk work and phone use can tighten these muscles. Massage eases that tension.  
- **Low back pain:** Targeted work helps loosen tight hip and back muscles.  
- **Headaches and TMJ:** Gentle massage can reduce jaw and scalp tension.  
- **Injury recovery:** Helps restore mobility after strains or accidents. (If you have a new injury, we will consult you or your doctor before treatment.)  
- **Wellness & relaxation:** Even without pain, many clients come simply to slow down, de-stress, and receive caring attention. As one source notes, massages can “reduce stress, relieve achy muscles, [and] improve your mood”.

Our goal is always your desired outcome: whether that’s pain relief, improved flexibility, or deep relaxation. You can request to focus on certain areas at any time, and we will adjust the plan to meet your goals.

## Who Can Benefit?  

Almost anyone can benefit from therapeutic massage. We tailor the session to your condition and comfort level. Commonly helped populations include:  

- **Athletes and active people** with chronic tightness or after workouts.  
- **Office workers** suffering from postural strain in the neck/back.  
- **Individuals with stress or anxiety** who need to relax their nervous system.  
- **Post-injury or post-surgery clients** (once cleared by a doctor) to aid recovery.  
- **Pregnant individuals** (using safe positioning and techniques) can often enjoy therapeutic massage. *(Always inform me of your pregnancy so I can make appropriate adjustments.)*  

Even if you **have no specific complaint**, a session can feel rejuvenating. As noted above, many clients simply come to make time for self-care and leave feeling refreshed.

## How Is a Session Planned?  

1. **Intake Conversation:** We start with a brief talk about your goals, health history, and any pain points.  
2. **Customized Plan:** Based on what you tell me, I choose techniques and areas of focus. I explain the plan before we begin.  
3. **Your Feedback:** Throughout the massage, I check in about pressure and comfort. Feel free to speak up or change focus at any time.  
4. **After-Care Advice:** When we finish, I’ll share tips (stretches, hydration, etc.) to maximize your results and answer any questions about follow-up sessions.

Our style is conversational and responsive. You know your body best; I combine that knowledge with 6+ years of training to guide the work. The session is truly a partnership.

## What To Expect During Your Appointment  

- **Preparation:** Please come hydrated. Wear comfortable clothing and be prepared to undress to your comfort level (sheet coverage provided). Your privacy and comfort are paramount.  
- **During Massage:** You may choose music or silence. We will use quality massage oils/lotions. You might feel light pressure all over, or deep work on problem areas – you are in control of intensity.  
- **Post-Massage:** You may feel relaxed, energized, or both. It’s normal to feel slightly drowsy or like you’ve had a gentle workout. Drink water after the session. If you have any questions about stretching or follow-up care, I’ll be happy to discuss them.

**Contraindications:** If you have any health condition (e.g. fever, acute injury, uncontrolled hypertension, recent surgery), please inform me before booking. New numbness, sharp or radiating pain, or severe illness may require medical clearance first. Massage is always adapted to your safety.

## Frequently Asked Questions  

**Q: What’s the difference between therapeutic and relaxation massage?**  
**A:** Relaxation (Swedish) massage focuses on soothing the whole body with gentle strokes. Therapeutic massage can also be soothing, but it gives extra attention to problem areas with techniques suited to your condition. It does *not* have to be painful – you set the comfort level.  

**Q: Will I feel results right away?**  
**A:** Everyone responds differently. Many clients feel immediate relief or relaxation, but for chronic issues it may take a few sessions to notice significant improvement. I will ask for feedback throughout and adjust the work to help you as best as possible.  

**Q: How often should I get a therapeutic massage?**  
**A:** There’s no fixed schedule. For acute issues, weekly sessions may help. For general wellness or stress relief, monthly or biweekly often works. We can discuss your goals and make a plan together.  

**Q: Can I get this massage if I’m pregnant?**  
**A:** Yes, we offer pregnancy-safe positioning (on side, with pillows) and gentle techniques. Please let me know how many weeks along you are, and I will adjust the session for your comfort and safety. Pregnancy massage can ease low back and hip tension.  

**Q: Is therapeutic massage covered by insurance?**  
**A:** Some medical insurance and HSA/FSA plans cover “massage therapy” or “medical massage.” Thai Solitude provides a receipt with the CPT code (assuming Oregon covers massage). It’s best to check with your provider about reimbursement.  

**Q: What should I wear?**  
**A:** Clients undress to their comfort level; you’ll be draped with a sheet. Many remove all clothing for easier access. Whatever you choose, we ensure your privacy. Pregnancy or modesty preferences are always respected.  

**Q: Can I focus only on my neck and shoulders?**  
**A:** Absolutely. Just tell me at the start (and any time during the massage) that you want extra attention on those areas. We will concentrate on your neck, shoulders, or any other problem spot. *(If your pain is new or severe, it’s wise to check with a doctor first.)*  

**Q: I’ve never had a therapeutic massage before. What should I do?**  
**A:** We’ll take it slow. I’ll explain everything before starting. You can ask any questions during the session. My priority is your comfort and trust. Many new clients are surprised at how pleasant targeted therapeutic work can feel.
